# Price Calculator & Fair-Trade Rules

## Overview
Transparent pricing engine providing fair compensation to rural producers while calculating transportation, cooperative fees, and tourism taxes.

## Components
1. **Producer Base Price**: Minimum floor price guaranteed to farmers/artisans.
2. **Cooperative / Enterprise Margin**: Aggregation and grading surcharge.
3. **Logistics & Delivery Fee**: Distance, road condition, and fuel calculation.
4. **Platform & Payment Fee**: Telebirr/processing fee (1-2%).
5. **Community Tourism Fund**: Optional contribution to South Omo local cultural preservation funds.
